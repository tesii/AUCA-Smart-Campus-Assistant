# AUCA Smart Campus Assistant

## Overview

AUCA Smart Campus Assistant is an AI powered web application designed to help students and staff quickly access university staff information and campus support services.

The system addresses a common challenge faced by new students and newly appointed staff members who may not know the appropriate office, department, or personnel responsible for specific services. Through a conversational chatbot interface, users can ask questions in natural language and receive relevant information about university staff, including their names, roles, departments, office locations, and contact details.

The platform improves accessibility to institutional information, reduces confusion, and enhances communication within the university community.

   

## Problem Statement

New students and newly appointed staff often face difficulties identifying the correct person or office responsible for handling specific requests such as academic matters, registration, finance, ICT support, or administrative services.

This results in:

* Delays in obtaining assistance
* Confusion regarding staff responsibilities
* Increased workload for reception and administrative offices
* Poor user experience for newcomers

The AUCA Smart Campus Assistant provides an intelligent solution by allowing users to ask questions and instantly receive accurate staff and department information.

## System Architecture

The application follows a client server architecture:

* Angular Frontend provides the chatbot interface.
* Spring Boot Backend processes user requests and business logic.
* REST APIs facilitate communication between frontend and backend.
* MySQL stores staff information and university data.
